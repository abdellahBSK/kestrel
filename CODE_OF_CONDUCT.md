# Code of Conduct

Kestrel adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
as its code of conduct. The full text at that link applies to every project space: issues, pull
requests, discussions and any other channel run by the project.

## In short

We want Kestrel to be a welcoming project for people of every background and experience level.

**Do:** be kind and patient, assume good intent, give and accept constructive feedback, credit
others' work, and focus on what is best for the people who use Kestrel.

**Don't:** harass, insult or demean anyone; use sexualized language or imagery; publish others'
private information; or keep up any behavior after being asked to stop.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the maintainers through
[GitHub's private reporting](../../security/advisories/new) or by contacting a maintainer
listed in [`.github/CODEOWNERS`](.github/CODEOWNERS). Every report is reviewed and kept
confidential. Maintainers may remove comments, commits or contributions, and may ban
contributors temporarily or permanently, following the Covenant's enforcement guidelines.
